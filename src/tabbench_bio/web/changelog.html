Benchmark updates.

27 November 2026

Causilo 1.5

Added Causilo 1.5 to the benchmark.

24 November 2026

TabOmics and Lattice

Added TabOmics, a new tabular data foundation model for high dimensional gene expression data and Lattice, a general TFM, to the benchmark.

LitePFN

Added LitePFN 1.5 to the benchmark.

21 October 2026

New datasets

Added the Tox21 SR-MMP and NR-AhR molecular property tasks and the ST001000 stool metabolomics dataset comparing Crohn's disease with ulcerative colitis.

14 October 2026

Failure Map and MLP

Added a failure map view that visualizes the failure rates at different feature and sample budgets. A tuned version of MLP was also added.

08 October 2026

LimiX-2 and GeneICL

Added LimiX-2 and GeneICL models.

01 October 2026

New (and tuned) models

Added Causalio, Kumo, TabPFN 3.5 and tuned variants of classical ml models.

30 September 2026

Probability metrics

Probability metrics retain the complete labelled class space. ROC-AUC is undefined when a test fold lacks a task class; those folds are omitted from ROC-AUC Elo. Rebuilding reports can therefore change ROC-AUC rankings and published numbers. Log loss uses every probability column without dropping absent test classes.

22 September 2026

Model cards

Added detailed visualisations of each model's strengths and weaknesses across dataset modalities and training budgets.

21 September 2026

Dataset explorer

Added improved per-dataset model comparisons and learning curves across feature and sample budgets.

20 August 2026

Initial release

Published the first TabBench-Bio benchmark website, interactive results, dataset registry, and versioned result artifacts.
